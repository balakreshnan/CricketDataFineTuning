Architecture: how the pieces fit together

Two models sit on the same GPU and share one tokenizer. The draft writes guesses, the target checks them, and saved copies of each model's cache make it possible to undo a rejected round. The animation steps through one example round with 4 guesses, of which the third is rejected. Click a step to stop on it.

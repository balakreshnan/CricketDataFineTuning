RLVR on a dense model and a mixture-of-experts model, compared

Two Qwen models were trained with reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) on the same T20 cricket questions, on 32 Vera Rubin GPUs of the Rhea cluster. Qwen3.8-27B is a dense model (run of 14 November 2026). Qwen3.6-35B-A3B is a mixture-of-experts model (run of 13 November 2026). Both runs logged utilisation, memory and power on every GPU every 15 seconds. This page compares the models, the training, the benchmark results and the GPU usage, minute by minute.

The two model architectures

Both models share the same layer design: three linear-attention layers followed by one full-attention layer, repeated. They differ in the feed-forward part of each layer. The diagrams show one layer; the animation shows which feed-forward weights a token uses.

How RLVR worked here

No worked solutions were used as training targets. The model answered questions itself, and a checker compared its final number with the exact answer.

The two recipes

Same method and dataset. The settings differ, so this is a comparison of two recipes, not a controlled experiment.

Fine-tuning Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B for cricket reasoning

Full-parameter SFT of NVIDIA's hybrid Mamba–Transformer MoE on 458K verified T20 reasoning traces with Megatron-Bridge. One epoch on 16 H100s took 1 h 37 min. On 600 questions from held-out matches, accuracy rose from 89.6% to 97.0%, and answers got 69% shorter.

Model: Balab2021/Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-Cricket-Reasoning · Data: CricketData-T20-Reasoning-Qwen3.8-Full · Metrics: W&B run

End-to-end pipeline

Each stage ran as a Slurm job in the NeMo 26.08.01 container. Code ran from ~/clustercodes, and all weights, caches and checkpoints lived on Lustre. Press play, or click a stage.

Model architecture

The 52 layers interleave Mamba-2 state-space mixers, sparse MoE feed-forwards and a few grouped-query attention layers, and an MTP head predicts two extra tokens. 30B parameters total, about 3B active per token. Click a layer for details.

Parallelism on 16 H100s

Dense and Mamba weights are split across GPU pairs (TP2 with sequence parallel). The 128 experts are spread over the 8 GPUs of a node (EP8, 16 experts per GPU). That leaves data parallelism of 8 for dense weights and 2 for experts. Click through one training step.

Data and sequence packing

Each row becomes a system/user/assistant chat with the trace as reasoning_content, so Nemotron's own template renders <think>…</think>. Examples are packed into 4,096-token sequences, about 6.25 per pack with 97.7% of each pack used. Loss counts only on the assistant turn.

Finding the cluster config

I benchmarked three parallel layouts for 25 steps each on 2 nodes. Only tensor parallelism of 2 fits in an 80 GB H100. I picked 2 nodes because the interactive partition starts 2-node jobs within minutes, while batch had a ~8 h queue.

Benchmark results

The fixed 600-question benchmark comes from 177 matches held out of training. Each question was sampled 4 times in thinking mode (T=1.0, top-p 0.95, ≤6,144 tokens) with vLLM. An answer scores if its last Final answer: line after </think> matches the exact value (rates within 0.01).
